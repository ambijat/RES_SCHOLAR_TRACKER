Privacy Policy

RES Scholar Tracker

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

Introduction

RES Scholar Tracker ("App") is an academic supervision and research administration tool designed for PhD researchers and their advisors. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Student and Advisor Information: Name, student ID, contact details, institutional affiliation
  • Academic Records: Research outline approvals, chapter submissions, RAC (Research Advisory Committee) meeting records, thesis status
  • Financial Records: Bill signatures (HRA, Fellowship, Contingency), receipt uploads, transaction history, payment status
  • Meeting Notes: Committee feedback, decisions, next meeting dates, supervision logs

1.2 Device Information

  • Device type, operating system, device identifier
  • App version and usage patterns
  • Crash logs and error reports only if collected by the app store or device platform

1.3 Location Information

We do not actively collect location data. Location is never tracked or stored.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

  • Supervision Management: Recording academic progress, milestone tracking, and approval workflows
  • Financial Administration: Tracking bill signatures and financial compliance
  • Institutional Compliance: Maintaining records required by your institution
  • App Improvement: Using exported feedback or manually shared reports to enhance functionality
  • Security: Keeping records local unless you choose to export or share them

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Data Storage

  • Student, document, publication, and activity records are stored locally in the browser/WebView storage on your device
  • The app includes bundled CSV seed files for initial student, document, publication, and message-template lists
  • Exports are created only when you tap export, download, copy, or share

3.2 Security Measures

  • No account login or cloud database is required for the local app workflow
  • Data stays on the device unless you explicitly export or share it
  • Protect device access with your operating system lock screen and backup controls
  • Keep the app updated when new builds are released

3.3 Data Retention

  • Local records remain on your device until you edit, delete, clear browser/app data, or uninstall the app
  • Exported files are controlled by whoever stores or receives those files
  • Institutional retention rules apply only after you submit or share exported records outside the app

4. Who We Share Your Data With

4.1 Data Sharing

We do NOT share your data with third parties except:
  • Your Institution: As required by your university's administrative systems (research office, finance office)
  • Selected Recipients: People or systems you choose when exporting, copying, downloading, or sharing records
  • Law Enforcement: When legally required by court order or regulatory authority

4.2 Committee Access

  • Research advisors can view records only on devices or exported files they control
  • Committee members can view records only when the advisor shares them outside the app
  • Students can view their own records only when the advisor shares them outside the app

5. Your Privacy Rights

5.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the App
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to institutional record retention policies)
  • Export your data in a portable format

5.2 Data Subject Requests

Use the app's export and delete controls for local records. For records already shared with an institution, contact the institution's data protection officer.

6. Children's Privacy

RES Scholar Tracker is intended for use by adult researchers (graduate students age 18+) and faculty advisors. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If we become aware of such collection, we will take immediate steps to delete the information.

7. Third-Party Services

7.1 Local WebView App

  • HTML app shell: Runs the tracker interface locally
  • Browser/WebView storage: Stores records on the device
  • Android share/download bridge: Saves or shares exports only when requested

7.2 Google Play Services

The app may be distributed through Google Play, which can provide platform-level services such as:

  • In-app updates
  • Security updates
  • Store listing and install metrics handled by Google Play

Google's Privacy Policy

8. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The app does not include a custom cloud analytics SDK. Platform-level crash or install data may still be handled by the app store or operating system.

9. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will:

  • Notify affected users within 48 hours
  • Provide information about the breach and protective measures taken
  • Offer identity protection services if applicable

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected, used, and shared
  • Delete personal information collected from you
  • Opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

Submit requests at: [In-app settings] → Privacy → Data Access Request

11. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access, rectification, and erasure of your data
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority

The legal basis for processing is:

  • Legitimate Interest: Institutional research administration
  • Contractual Necessity: Supervising your academic progress
  • Consent: For optional features (messaging, notifications)

Data Protection Officer contact: Your institution's Data Protection Officer

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes via:

  • In-app notification
  • Email to your registered address
  • Updated "Last Updated" date on this policy

Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
